# Two strategies for improving machine translation from Tibetan

**What it is:** a 15-page method note by Drupchen, written for translators of spoken Tibetan teachings into French. It is kept in the knowledge base [Papers](https://buddhistai.github.io/knowledge-base/papers/two-strategies-for-machine-translation-from-tibetan/README.html), with the full text in Markdown and the author's PDF. This card only links to it.

**Who it is for:** translators from Tibetan, especially into French.

## How to use it

Read the short summary on the Papers page first. To use it with an AI, open the [full text in Markdown](https://github.com/BuddhistAI/knowledge-base/blob/main/papers/two-strategies-for-machine-translation-from-tibetan/paper.md), click **Copy raw file**, and paste it into a Claude Project's instructions or at the start of a chat. It is long, so the AI follows it best when you also answer its five context questions.

## Skills made from it

Two skills follow this method and will be measured: the variants `drupchen-method-units-v1` and `drupchen-method-onepass-v1` in [translation-tibetan-to-french](https://github.com/BuddhistAI/translation-tibetan-to-french). Drupchen did not write those skills.
